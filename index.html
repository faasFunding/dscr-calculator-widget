Free DSCR Calculator Widget

Check how a rental's rent covers its full monthly payment (principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA). Add it to any website with one line of code.

Add it to your site

<div id="faas-dscr-calculator"></div>
<script src="https://faasfunding.com/embed/dscr-calculator.js" async></script>

Change the button color with data-accent:

<div class="faas-dscr-widget" data-accent="#1D3366"></div>

The widget runs in a Shadow DOM, so your site's styles won't break it. It collects no personal data and sets no cookies.
